## Story

> Oh, oh, you want the good stuff, the loud stuff, yes? Listen, listen, before you touch anything that ticks: say the word and Engineering and Alchemy become your two majors, the only two you get to push past rare work (that is where it gets FUN, trust me). The craft opposite goes in your pocket as a hobby, rare and no further, do not pout. Your other trades? Not gone, <your name>, just napping, dormant, wake them whenever you like. But (there is always a but, hold the fuse) ditch this pair and waddle back later and it costs you sweat that piles up, five things put down the first time, eight the next, eleven after, more, more, every single time you get cold feet. Yes? YES? Then go pick me three patches of herbs, the volatile ones, do not ask which, they are all a little volatile if you believe hard enough.
